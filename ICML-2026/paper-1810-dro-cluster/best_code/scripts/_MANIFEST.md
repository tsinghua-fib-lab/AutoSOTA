Command-line entry points for reproducing paper experiments.
- `run_ablation_studies.py`: runs rho, K, and alpha ablations into CSV summaries with configurable ADMM iteration budget.
- `run_face_experiments.py`: runs source-grounded Extended Yale B face clustering experiments, including MFC variants.
- `run_synthetic_experiments.py`: runs source-grounded synthetic main, sweep, and additional scenarios, including MFC variants.
