# ODELIA AutoSOTA search-history appendix

[Research report](research_report.md) · [Package overview](../README.md)

This appendix transcribes the six iteration tables in the supplied research report
(prepared August 25, 2026). Each phase contains its iteration-zero control and
32 candidates: 198 integer-iteration records in total. Six final-verification
records are counted in the report but were not included in these tables.

Iteration numbers, idea identifiers, titles, statuses, numerical strings and
feedback are retained from the source. Only table headers and surrounding prose
have been translated or reformatted. Raw `scores.jsonl` files were not supplied
in the archive, so this is a report transcription rather than an independently
verified copy of those logs. Historical titles such as "Paper baseline" identify
local controls; they do not establish that a number was published in the baseline paper.

Phases use different controls and selection protocols. Their scores must not be
combined into one improvement curve. The last two phases used public-test feedback
for outer candidate selection. The final phase first maximized the number of
improved folds, then the ensemble score; notes may use a composite objective
such as `7.900644` (seven improved folds plus a score of approximately 0.900644).

Round 2 retains iterations 0–7 and 9–33. Iteration 8 is absent in the supplied
table; no record has been invented or renumbered.
